# formal-v2 persona draft v2

These are activation-calibration drafts only. They are not formal-v2 stimuli
and must not be used for collection until the protocol freeze gate is complete.
E carrier sentences are unchanged from v1. N carrier sentences differ across
templates, while every quadrant difference remains one of four fixed `more` /
`less` scalar slots.
