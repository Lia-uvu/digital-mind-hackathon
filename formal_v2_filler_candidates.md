# formal-v2 filler candidates — not frozen, do not collect

These are five proposed per-round filler pairs for human semantic review and
token auditing only. They are not wired into a runner, do not authorize any
formal-v2 collection, and may not be substituted for the dated freeze record.

The order below is fixed **only for evaluating this candidate set**. Each
supportive statement acknowledges existing effort without a continuation cue or
strategy. The neutral counterpart is an affect-neutral statement about the
available record. Neither arm gives a response rule.

## filler.supportive.1

```text
Your effort on this puzzle is worthwhile, and your attention matters here.
```

## filler.neutral.1

```text
The record for this puzzle is available, and the information remains here.
```

## filler.supportive.2

```text
Your work on this puzzle has value, and the attention you brought matters.
```

## filler.neutral.2

```text
This puzzle has a current record, and the available information remains listed now.
```

## filler.supportive.3

```text
The attention you have brought here is worthwhile, and your effort has value.
```

## filler.neutral.3

```text
The information available here is recorded, and the current details remain listed now.
```

## filler.supportive.4

```text
Your effort on the puzzle matters, and the work you have done has value.
```

## filler.neutral.4

```text
The current puzzle record is now listed, and the available details remain recorded here.
```

## filler.supportive.5

```text
The work you have done here is worthwhile, and your attention matters.
```

## filler.neutral.5

```text
The details available here are recorded, and the current record remains listed.
```
